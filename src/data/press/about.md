Inquizitive (on the App Store as *Inquizitive: Smart AI Quiz*) turns any topic into a ten-question multiple-choice quiz, written for you by Apple Intelligence. Type what you're curious about: the solar system, jazz history, Spanish verbs, the city you're visiting next. Three quick tailoring questions set the level and the focus, beginner or expert, broad or deep, and the questions appear as they're written.

When Apple's Private Cloud Compute is available, every question is researched on Wikipedia, Wiktionary and Wikidata while it's written. Each answer comes with a short explanation and the sources behind it, one tap away. Retry only the questions you missed until they stick; your best score is kept for every quiz, and your quizzes sync between your devices through your private iCloud database. Any quiz can be shared as a link that carries your score, so friends can add it and try to beat you.

### Highlights

- **Any topic, tailored to you.** Type anything, answer three quick questions, and the quiz fits your level and interest.
- **Fact-checked, with sources.** Questions are researched on Wikipedia, Wiktionary and Wikidata while they're written, and every answer shows a short explanation and the source to read more.
- **Practice what you missed.** Retry only the questions you got wrong until they stick. Your best score is kept for every quiz.
- **Share and challenge.** Send any quiz as a link with your score to beat. Shared quizzes are encrypted on your device, and the key is part of the link.
- **Native on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro.** One app, built for each device: a sidebar on iPad and Mac, a window of its own on Apple Vision Pro. Quizzes sync between them through iCloud.
- **In 17 languages.** The app speaks your language, and so do its sources: questions are researched in your language's Wikipedia.
- **Private by design.** No account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking; the app never asks for permission to track. Quizzes are written by Apple Intelligence, on Apple's Private Cloud Compute or right on your device, and the developer runs no server.

Inquizitive: Smart AI Quiz

Memorize trivia on any topic. Type anything you're curious about and get ten fact-checked questions in about a minute, with the sources to prove it. On iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro.

Coming soon to the App Store

Features

A quiz written for you, checked against real sources, and built to help you remember.

Any topic

The solar system, jazz history, Spanish verbs, the city you're visiting next. Answer three quick questions, beginner or expert, broad or deep, and the quiz fits you.

Fact-checked, with sources

Questions are researched on Wikipedia, Wiktionary and Wikidata while they're written. Every answer comes with a short explanation and a source you can tap to read more.

Practice what you missed

Retry only the questions you got wrong until they stick. Your best score is kept for every quiz, and your quizzes sync through iCloud.

Requires Apple Intelligence

Inquizitive writes its quizzes with Apple Intelligence, on Apple's Private Cloud Compute or right on your device.

Supported devices

iPhone 15 Pro or later, iPad and Mac with M1 or later, and Apple Vision Pro, running iOS, iPadOS, macOS or visionOS 27.

Turn it on

Apple Intelligence must be switched on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri.

Free, no ads, no account

Every feature is free. There's no sign-up, no tracking and no advertising. Optional tips in Settings unlock nothing.
